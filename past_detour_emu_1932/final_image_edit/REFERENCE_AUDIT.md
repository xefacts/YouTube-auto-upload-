# Reference review: user-supplied Text and Train folders

Reviewed 13 screenshots in the **Text** folder and sampled across all 16 video files in the **Train** root/demo subfolder (15 distinct videos because the 10-second 16:9 demo is duplicated). This is a motion/typography reference audit only. No reference footage, narration, screenshot UI, watermark, logo or third-party character has been copied into the new film. Files were inspected locally, not included in the delivered source.

## Text screenshots (13)

The screenshots repeatedly show oversized high-contrast condensed sans type, a restrained electric-lime accent on key words, and type positioned as part of the scene behind/near a speaker rather than boxed spoken subtitles. A few include editorial serif contrast and stacked date-like type. We use licensed Anton/DM Sans fonts, warm cream + muted lime against teal/ochre scenery, and only in-image story facts already in the Phase 2 narration: `NOVEMBER 1932`, the reported `986`, the contemporary nickname `EMU WAR`, `1934` (already printed by the image model on the shop wall), and `57,000+` claims. No timed subtitle layer.

## Resulting camera rules

1. Script and 48-second mixed audio stay the same. Pans/dollies are shot-specific and ease in/out; no repeated identical Ken Burns zoom.
2. Nine newly generated alternate stills change faces, gestures, bird spacing, a page-hand and wire tension at matched camera angles. They are **image cuts**, not hand-rigged character animation.
3. The virtual camera tilts up from the 1932 birds into an all-sky generated still, then tilts down into an empty Wheatbelt field before the existing 1934 supply scene. This is the one intentional time/location change.
4. Typography is baked into scene stills before video encoding. The final film contains no ASS/SRT overlay, subtitle band, chapter tag or borrowed reference elements.
