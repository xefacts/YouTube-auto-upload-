## Editorial standards reflected in the copy
- Do not claim to solve an open case absent reliable official resolution.
- Distinguish established facts, attributed allegations, theories, and unknowns.
- Treat victims, families, and suspects carefully; do not imply guilt without adjudicated evidence.
- Mark dramatizations/illustrations as recreations, never as archival evidence.
- Cite trustworthy sources in each episode description and correct material errors.
