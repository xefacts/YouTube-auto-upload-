# CLUEFIELD — Channel brand proposal

## Identity
- **Display name:** CLUEFIELD (spoken/written as Cluefield)
- **Tagline:** TRUE STORIES. HIDDEN DETAILS.
- **Positioning:** concise, evidence-led explainers about real historical mysteries, heists, covert operations and consequential blunders.
- **Voice:** curious, composed, intelligent and approachable—not sensational or falsely certain.
- **Palette:** midnight navy `#08121B`, petrol `#0A1C27`, warm ivory `#F7F3E3`, muted copper `#CD9153`, sea-glass teal `#75BFB7`.
- **Typography:** Anton for display words and DM Sans for readable supporting copy. Font OFL notices reside in `../assets/fonts/`.
- **Mascot:** original illustrated male explainer; secondary to the wordmark, never imply he is a real witness.

## Why the name works
One memorable word, two clear syllables, easy to say and spell; “clue” signals curiosity while “field” feels broad enough for history, geography and discovery. It can sit cleanly in a banner, thumbnail or sponsor deck without locking the project into murder/crime-only branding. Name and handle still need independent clearance and YouTube Studio verification; this is not legal trademark advice.
