The Mona Lisa disappeared from the Louvre in 1911. The investigation lasted more than two years—until the painting was offered to a Florence art dealer. This short follows the evidence, the conviction, and the unresolved question of motive.

Vincenzo Peruggia was convicted in Florence in 1914 and sentenced to one year and fifteen days. He claimed he wanted to return the painting to Italy; his attempt to sell it leaves motive open to interpretation. This episode does not claim to solve anything beyond the established conviction.

Visual note: all period scenes in this video are original illustrative reconstructions, not archival footage. No graphic imagery is used.
