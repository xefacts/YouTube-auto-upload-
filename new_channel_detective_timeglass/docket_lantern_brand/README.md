# THE DOCKET LANTERN — YouTube channel brand kit (proposal)

**Decision-stage concept, not a live-channel update.** This kit responds to the user's request to stop work on branded intros and receive one professional channel name, high-quality profile, banner, description, and their upload-ready files in one folder. The older **Arlen Archive / Finch intro names are superseded as channel-name proposals**. The selected freckled ginger male detective remains a visual mascot; his personal name is not decided here. Existing gold/Sona channel untouched.

## Selected name, tagline and handle

- **Channel display name proposal:** **The Docket Lantern**. *Docket* evokes a case record; *Lantern* evokes revealing a detail. Short, distinctive, easy to set as a strong two-line editorial wordmark; fits historical heists, covert operations, mysteries and tactical blunders.
- **Banner tagline:** **HISTORY LEAVES CLUES**.
- **YouTube handle candidate (not reserved / not verified):** `@TheDocketLantern`. On 2026-11-01 the public URL `https://www.youtube.com/@TheDocketLantern` returned a 404, **which does not establish that the handle is claimable**. Only check in YouTube Studio after the user approves this brand; do not change or claim it autonomously. Search results cannot prove exclusivity or trademark clearance either.

## YouTube sizing and continuity checks

YouTube recommends **2560 × 1440 px** banner, upload ≤**6 MB**, and reports a centered **1235 × 338 px** safe area at the 2048 × 1152 minimum; scaled to this recommended banner, the safe area is approximately **1544 × 423 px**. We use a centered **1546 × 423 px** review crop (x=507..2053, y=508..931) and keep the essential text and logo comfortably within it. Profile can be JPG/PNG/GIF/BMP (not animated GIF), ≤15 MB and renders at 98 × 98 px. See https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12950272?hl=en . Check YouTube's *own preview* before making any approved change because display crops vary.

The artwork does not modify either channel or YouTube. Name, description, handle and profile/banner still require the user's explicit approval before anyone touches a live account. This folder is a self-contained review package, **not a published channel**.
