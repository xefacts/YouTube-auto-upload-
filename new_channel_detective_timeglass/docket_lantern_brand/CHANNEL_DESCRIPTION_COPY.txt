The Docket Lantern follows the evidence through history’s strangest turns: daring heists, vanished crews, covert operations, and tactical blunders.

Each short case separates documented facts from disputed theories. When original footage or photographs don’t exist, we use clearly labeled original illustrations—never pass reconstructions off as archival evidence. Some mysteries remain unresolved, and we’ll tell you when the record runs out.
