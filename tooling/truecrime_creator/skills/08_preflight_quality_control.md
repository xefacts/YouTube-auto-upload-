# Skill 08 — Release preflight

## Facts and ethics
- Every factual sentence has a source and attribution; exact date/place/name/age/legal outcome verified.
- Allegations are not described as facts; motive is attributed unless established; uncertainty acknowledged.
- No copied competitor footage, script, voice, images, music, logo or watermarks; licenses/provenance recorded.
- No unapproved suspect naming, doxxing, graphic content or active-case interference.

## Story and performance
- Hook is truthful and paid off; evidence arrives in understandable order; mid-viewer question is answered; final sentence intact.
- Narration is intelligible and natural on phone speaker; music/SFX beneath voice; pronunciation and emotional balance checked.

## Picture and export
- Play full timeline; review contact sheets; check every transition, captions, spelling, safe areas and final frame.
- Verify codec, resolution, aspect ratio, FPS, runtime, stream audio rate/channels, loudness, peak, decodability and file size.
- Run black/freeze detection and listen/view beginning, question beat, reveal, ending and a random midpoint.
- Include source notes, transcript/SRT, edit assets, licenses, checksums and private-review label.
- Upload owner-only for review; do not publish or modify a live channel without explicit authorization.
