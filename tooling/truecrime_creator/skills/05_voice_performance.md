# Skill 05 — Natural, emotionally controlled voice-over

## Free offline option used in this project
Kokoro q8 ONNX + `am_michael` voice tensors, Apache-2.0; works locally without an account/API key. The model hash is verified in `setup_v3_voice.py`. Synthesize short paragraph takes, audition at phone-speaker volume, then edit pauses; never clone a real person or competitor narrator.

## Direction sheet for TTS or a licensed ElevenLabs voice
- Voice: warm, grounded adult U.S. narrator; clear consonants; conversational chest register; no trailer-announcer rasp.
- Hook: restrained urgency and curiosity; do not shout.
- Evidence: steady, precise and neutral.
- Midpoint audience question: direct and slightly slower, with a beat afterward.
- Verdict/sentence: plain, non-triumphal and compassionate.
- Final question: open curiosity; no fake certainty.
- Delivery target: approximately 135–155 wpm for a documentary Short; use real pauses and keep the final word intact.

## Emotional tuning
Use punctuation, sentence length, breath gaps, multiple takes and mild speed changes. Keep emotion grounded in the facts. Do not tell a voice model to impersonate a named host, victim, suspect, family member, law-enforcement officer or reference-video speaker. Check pronunciation of names and listen to the full mix before release.
