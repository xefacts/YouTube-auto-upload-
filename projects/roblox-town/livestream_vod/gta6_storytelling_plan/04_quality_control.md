# 04 — Final Quality-Control Checklist (Section K)

Status: checklist only. Nothing has been checked against a finished video. Each item is pass/fail once production runs.

## Gate 0 — before any editing (all must pass)

- [ ] Written permission for third-party footage, saved in the repo with scope, date and expiry (README decision 1)
- [ ] Early-access/embargo question answered (README decision 2)
- [ ] Blur and mute policy confirmed (README decision 3)
- [ ] Voice system confirmed: Arena voices, no ElevenLabs (README decision 4)
- [ ] Caption rule confirmed (README decision 5) and length confirmed (README decision 6)
- [ ] Public links for v2/v3 reviewed and restricted if needed

## Gate 1 — story accuracy

- [ ] Every mission name appears on screen (HUD or title) in the footage, with timestamp
- [ ] Every story claim traces to a timestamp or a verified source in `02_story_and_chronology.md`
- [ ] No invented dialogue presented as game dialogue
- [ ] Event order matches the footage; no reordering that changes meaning
- [ ] Launch date and status checked against official Rockstar sources (date conflict in `02`)

## Gate 2 — content and rights

- [ ] No unlicensed music anywhere in the timeline
- [ ] No watermark, attribution, or owner information cropped out
- [ ] Profanity muted; no explicit or graphic material; no sexualized scenes
- [ ] No other people's PII (chat names, face cam) visible without permission
- [ ] Thumbnail matches the video; no misleading claims

## Gate 3 — technical

- [ ] 1920×1080, 30 or 60 fps, H.264 MP4, audio loudness consistent; narration audible over game audio
- [ ] Duration within 25:00–30:00 (target ~28:00)
- [ ] Gameplay share 70–85% of runtime
- [ ] Captions and labels do not cover HUD, objectives, subtitles, minimap or notifications
- [ ] Final file checked by playing it end to end, not just by metadata
