## 2. Story Arc & 4-Minute Survival Rule

- **Premise:** An airborne-fast rage pathogen erupts in the city below. From the instant the Emergency Alert hits your phone, it takes **240 seconds (`04:00`)** for the wave to reach your stairwell.
- **Core Hook:** Almost everyone wastes the first **90 seconds** looking out the window or trying to drive out into gridlock. Those 4 minutes decide everything: **Water → Barricade → Silence → Elevation**.
