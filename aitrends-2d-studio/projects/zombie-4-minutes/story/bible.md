# Story Bible — "What If a Zombie Apocalypse Started and You Only Had 4 Minutes?"

## Logline & Tone
High-tension second-person survival thriller + science breakdown: the emergency siren sounds and you have **exactly 4 minutes (240 seconds)** before the infected reach your floor. Every 60 seconds has one non-negotiable survival rule.

## 5 Visual Styles Showcased
1. **Cinematic Dusk (`dusk`)**: Mauve-purple shadows, warm amber sunset key light, cream rim light.
2. **Midnight Blue (`midnight`)**: Cold navy apocalypse night, icy blue-white rim light, sodium streetlamps.
3. **Golden Hour (`golden`)**: Scorched orange-and-teal twilight outbreak, blazing horizon sun.
4. **Neon Noir (`neon`)**: Deepest blacks, cyberpunk magenta & cyan rim lights, intense bloom.
5. **Soft Storybook (`storybook`)**: Stylized graphic-novel pastel dusk, lavender shadows, crisp paper grain.
