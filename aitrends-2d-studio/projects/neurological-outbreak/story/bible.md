# Story Bible — Neurological Outbreak ("You Are Inside Aisle Four")

## 1. Visual Style Preset (Chosen)
- **Preset:** `midnight` (**Midnight Blue**)
  - Shadows: Deep cold slate navy (`#0b1424`)
  - Rim light: Icy lunar blue-white (`#c9dcff`)
  - Practicals: Warm flickering sodium/fluorescent convenience-store light (`#ffd089`), emergency red alert strobes (`#ff4f5e`), and sickly underground maintenance green (`#4de68a`).
  - Why: Psychological horror + isolation thriller set from dusk through midnight to cold grey dawn and underground tunnels.

## 2. Dual-Ratio Hybrid Architecture (`16:9` Long-Form + `9:16` Vertical Shorts Series)
- **Master Canvas:** `1920 × 1080` (`16:9` Full HD @ `24fps`), ZERO burned-in subtitles (clean cinema).
- **Center-Safe `9:16` Column:** `x = 656 .. 1264` (`width = 608 px`, `height = 1080 px` — exact `9:16` ratio!).
  - **Inner Action Safe Zone (`x = 685 .. 1235`):** ALL key story beats — `"YOU"` protagonist, first-person POV hands/phone/flashlight, infected silhouettes at doors & broken windows, inventory monitor, offline transit map, evacuation wall map, Control Room Man, and Silent Boy — are composed inside this central column.
  - **16:9 Atmospheric Wings (`0 .. 656` & `1264 .. 1920`):** Convenience store shelves, flickering refrigerators, crashed intersection cars, abandoned bus, concrete tunnel pipework, and volumetric shadows fill the horizontal periphery so the `16:9` master looks expansive and theatrical while center-cropped `1080 × 1920` (`9:16`) exports yield **6 native-looking Vertical YouTube Shorts** with zero clipped subjects or UI!

## 3. Episodic Shorts Series Structure (6 Natural Cliffhanger Chapters)
1. **Short / Episode 1 (`Part 1`):** *Aisle Four & The Biological Emergency* — Soup cans → vibrating phone → chase outside → locking deadbolt → delivery driver runs → pulling steel shutters down.
2. **Short / Episode 2 (`Part 2`):** *Half Stock & The Woman at the Glass* — Dead phone lines → flickering fridges → missing deliveries on office PC → rationing → motionless woman mouths *"Help me"* as a twitching figure appears behind her.
3. **Short / Episode 3 (`Part 3`):** *The Back Storage Window* — Nightfall → barricading front doors → rusted back delivery door + hand truck & cargo strap → clatter in the dark → broken wire-mesh glass, searching arm & low clicking sound → retreat to main floor.
4. **Short / Episode 4 (`Part 4`):** *Midnight Map & The Ringing Phone* — Shaking hands at midnight → radio static → offline map to Municipal Transit Station 2 blocks east → eerie phone ringing in an infected's pocket outside the shelves.
5. **Short / Episode 5 (`Part 5`):** *Crossing the Dead Street* — Packing backpack at dawn → rear door breached → silent street, abandoned bus, infected facing the station → child's backpack & family photo → chased down station stairs → `"Are you alone?"` → `"No."` → pulled inside!
6. **Short / Episode 6 (`Part 6`):** *Control Room — "That's Not My Dad"* — Evacuation map wall → green maintenance corridor → radio static + frightened child `"Please don't open the door"` → three knocks → `"Dad?"` → looking down at the silent boy holding your jacket: **`"That's not my dad."`** → blackout + footsteps sprinting toward you!

## 4. Second-Person `"YOU"` Immersion Rules
- Audience IS the main character (`YOU`).
- Use **First-Person POV** framing (`your hands` holding soup cans, `your phone` buzzing with the national alert, `your hand` slamming the steel deadbolt, `your flashlight cone` cutting dark corridors, `your shaking hands` behind the counter, looking down at `the boy holding your jacket`) paired with **anonymous dark-slate hoodie over-the-shoulder/silhouette framing** (`YOU` never given a distracting name or third-person persona).
